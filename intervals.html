Confidence, prediction or tolerance interval?

Thirty fill weights, three intervals, three very different answers. Choosing the wrong one is how specifications end up too tight, or out-of-trend rules never fire.

Same mean, same SD, different multiplier

All three are the mean plus or minus a multiple of the SD. What changes is what the multiple has to cover.

CI: x̄ ± t · s / √n
PI: x̄ ± t · s · √(1 + 1/n)
TI: x̄ ± k · s, k = 3.351 for n = 30

The confidence interval shrinks as you add data, because it is only about the mean. The prediction interval never gets narrower than about ±2 SD, because one new vial still varies. The tolerance interval has to cover nearly every vial, so it is the widest of the three.

The common mistake: setting a specification from a confidence interval. Here that would put the limits at 5.003 and 5.027 g, and 20 of these 30 vials would fail.
